# HOOKS & RE-HOOKS

The first line decides whether the post lives. **Under 140 characters, under 12 words ideal.**

## Hook checklist, hit at least 2

- [ ] מספר ספציפי או data point (`100+`, `92%`, `₪10K`, `40%`)
- [ ] קריאה לזהות: `אם את [תפקיד]…`
- [ ] סותר אמונה רווחת: `תפסיקי לעשות X`, `כולם טועים לגבי…`
- [ ] Curiosity gap: מבטיח payoff, מסתיר אותו
- [ ] Borrowed authority: `[שם מוכר] עשה X. ככה:`
- [ ] קצר. משפט חלקי בסדר גמור.

## Hook formulas

**Treat these as shapes to fill.** Each one needs the voice owner's own specifics dropped in before it means anything. A formula rendered with generic content is a LinkedIn cliché and reads as one. If the voice owner has no real number or real story for a formula, pick a different formula rather than inventing the detail.

Log which formula each post used. Across a 3-post set, three different ones.

| Type | Hebrew shape | English shape |
|------|--------------|---------------|
| **Confession** | פיטרתי את [X] ש[מספר] | I fired [X] who [number] |
| **Authority / volume** | אחרי [N] [אנשים / פרויקטים], ראיתי את אותו הדפוס | After [N] [X], I noticed one pattern |
| **Identity POV** | כ[תפקיד], אני אגיד את האמת על [נושא] | As a [role], here's my honest take on [X] |
| **Result claim** | איך עברתי מ-[X] ל-[Y] ב-[זמן] | How I went from [X] to [Y] in [time] |
| **Mistake reveal** | הטעות ש-[N]% מ[קהל] עושים | The mistake [N]% of [audience] make |
| **Contrarian** | כולם אומרים [X]. אני עושה [Y] | Them: [X]. Me: [Y] |
| **Curiosity gap** | יש [שאלה / מבחן] אחד שאני שואלת ב[מצב]. הוא חושף הכול | There's one question I ask in every [X] |
| **Underdog** | [N] החודשים הראשונים שלי ב[תחום]? [תוצאה גרועה] | My first [N] months doing [X], I made [$0] |
| **Borrowed credibility** | [שם] עשה [הישג מדיד]. ככה: | [Name] did [measurable thing]. Here's how: |
| **Comeback frame** | ב-[שנה] [חברה] הייתה ב[מצב גרוע]. ב-[שנה] [מצב טוב]. איך? | In [year] [company] was [bad]. By [year], [good]. How? |
| **Cost reveal** | [ההחלטה] עלתה לי [מספר]. הייתי עושה אותה שוב | [Decision] cost me [number]. I'd do it again |

### Building a real hook from a shape

```
Shape:    אחרי [N] [אנשים], ראיתי את אותו הדפוס
Bad:      אחרי הרבה לקוחות, ראיתי דפוס מעניין
Better:   אחרי 40 שיחות מכירה, כולן נתקעו באותה שאלה
```

The second one fails because `הרבה` and `מעניין` are the writer refusing to commit. The third works because 40 is checkable and "the same question" opens a loop the reader wants closed.

## Hooks to avoid

- ״בעולם של היום…״ / "In today's world…"
- ״כולנו יודעים ש…״ / "As we all know…"
- ״אני רוצה לדבר על…״ / "I want to talk about…"
- Any long windup with subordinate clauses before the point
- Anything that promises no payoff
- Anything on Layer 1 of `voice-gate.md`

## THE RE-HOOK

One line, immediately after the hook. It deepens the loop and earns the "see more" click. Skipping it is the most common reason a strong hook produces no reach: the reader gets the punchline in line one and has no reason to expand. [rule of thumb]

Patterns:

- ״הנה מה שקרה:״ / "Here's what happened:"
- ״לקח לי [N] שנים להבין את זה.״ / "It took me [N] years to see it."
- ״אבל…״ / "Yet…"
- ״והבעיה לא הייתה מה שחשבתי.״ / "And the problem wasn't what I assumed."
- A claim that **escalates** the hook rather than explaining it.

Two rules:
1. The re-hook must not start with a negation (`voice-gate.md`, Layer 1).
2. The re-hook must not summarise the post. If a reader can stop after it and lose nothing, it is a payoff in the wrong place.

## BUILDING A HOOK BANK

Once there are posts in the feed, stop guessing. Create `references/hook-bank.md` and log every hook that outperformed, measured at a fixed interval so the rows compare to each other. 72 hours after publishing works well and is easy to keep up.

Copy this in as the starting file:

```markdown
# HOOK BANK: [voice owner]

Measured 72h after publishing. Median impressions across all posts: [fill in after 10 posts]

| Date | Hook (first line, verbatim) | Formula | Post type | Impressions | Comments | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-01-14 | | Contrarian | Curiosity | | | |

## Patterns so far
- [what the top 3 hooks have in common]
- [what the bottom 3 have in common]
- [any formula from hooks.md that consistently underperforms here, and a guess at why]
```

The Notes column carries the thing a number cannot: whether the post was topical that week, whether someone with reach commented early, whether the voice owner was tagged by a bigger account. A hook that looks like a winner because a 40k-follower account reshared it will mislead you for months.

After 10 to 15 entries the bank beats the table above, because it holds shapes that worked on **their** audience rather than on LinkedIn in general. Seed from the bank first and fall back to the table only when the topic fits nothing in it.
