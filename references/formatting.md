# FORMATTING & PRE-PUBLISH

## A note on the numbers in this file

Every figure below is marked `[rule of thumb]`. They work in practice and nobody measured them on your audience. They come from the LinkedIn creator community and from platform behaviour that changes without notice. None of them was measured on your audience.

Treat them as starting positions. Once you have 20 posts of real data, those numbers override every one of these, and you should write the real ones in here.

---

## Formatting rules

1. **One sentence, one line.** Whitespace is the layout. LinkedIn renders dense paragraphs as a grey wall and readers skip them.
2. **Vary line length.** Inside any block of 4 lines, at least one runs 15+ words and at least one runs under 8. Three same-length lines in a row is the staccato failure in `voice-gate.md`, and it is the single loudest AI tell in the format.
3. **A full blank line** between every 1 to 2 sentences.
4. **No block longer than 2 lines.** Break the block, not the sentence.
5. **Bullets (•) or arrows (→)** for any list of 3+ items.
6. **Length: 1,000 to 1,300 characters** for a text post. [rule of thumb]
7. **A question post runs shorter: 600 to 1,000 characters.** Over-explaining a question answers it and kills the comments. [rule of thumb]
8. **The link goes in the first comment**, never in the body. Posts with an external link in the body are widely reported to lose meaningful reach. [rule of thumb]
9. **Hashtags: 3 to 5, at the end, specific.** Broad tags (#marketing, #business) add nothing.
10. **Hebrew: one gender of address, held to the end.** See `hebrew.md`.
11. **First 3 lines carry the hook and re-hook.** The "see more" cut falls around line 3 on desktop and line 2 on mobile. [rule of thumb] Paste into the LinkedIn composer before approval and look at where it cuts.

## CTA strategy: soft beats hard

| CTA type | Use when | Effect |
|----------|----------|--------|
| **Soft** (״מחשבות?״ / "Thoughts?") | Story or opinion post | Highest reply volume [rule of thumb] |
| **Question** (״מסכימים או לא?״) | Contrarian post | Deepest comment threads [rule of thumb] |
| **Comment-to-DM** (״תכתבו ׳מסגרת׳ ואשלח״) | Lead gen, case study, resource | Highest qualified replies, use sparingly [rule of thumb] |
| **Save prompt** (״שמרו לפעם הבאה ש…״) | Authority frameworks | Saves are a strong ranking signal [rule of thumb] |
| **None** | Strong contrarian close | Relies on reshares. Works when the last line is the payoff itself |

**Avoid:** "DM me", "Click the link", ״תכנסו לקישור״. They ask for a platform-exit and reach drops. [rule of thumb]

Comment-to-DM more than once a fortnight trains the audience to read the voice owner as a funnel. Rotate it.

## PRE-PUBLISH CHECKLIST

Run every post through this. A single unchecked box is a rewrite.

**Structure**
- [ ] Hook ≤140 chars and hits 2+ items from the hook checklist
- [ ] Re-hook line sits immediately after the hook and escalates rather than explains
- [ ] ONE framework, held to the end
- [ ] Hook and re-hook both land above the "see more" cut
- [ ] No block longer than 2 lines
- [ ] Payoff line is explicit and is not something the reader already knew
- [ ] CTA is soft, question, comment-to-DM or save
- [ ] Length 1,000 to 1,300 chars, or 600 to 1,000 for a question post
- [ ] Link is in the first comment, not the body
- [ ] Post is classified Curiosity, Authority or Vulnerability

**Voice**
- [ ] `voice-gate.md` Layer 1 run, zero hits
- [ ] `voice-gate.md` Layer 2 run against the voice owner block
- [ ] No three same-length lines in a row
- [ ] Reads out loud like someone talking, not someone presenting
- [ ] Hebrew: `hebrew.md` run, gender consistent, gershayim ״״, digits for numbers

**Substance**
- [ ] Every number is either supplied by the voice owner or marked `[מספר חסר, לאמת]`
- [ ] Specific nouns where adjectives were doing the work
- [ ] At least one detail only the voice owner could have written
- [ ] No tagged person without the voice owner's explicit confirmation

## DELIVERY

Hand over the draft, the classification, the first comment, and an explicit list of anything unverified. Then stop and let the voice owner decide whether it goes up.
