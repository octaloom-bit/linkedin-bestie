# VOICE GATE

Load before writing. Run again on the finished post. Every hit sends the draft back for a rewrite, however small it looks on the page.

Two layers. **Layer 1 is universal** and fires for everyone in every language. **Layer 2 belongs to the voice owner**, which by default is you, and you fill it in STEP 0.

---

## LAYER 1. UNIVERSAL AI TELLS (auto-fail)

These patterns make a post read machine-written no matter whose name is on it.

### Rhythm

This is the section people skip and it is the one that decides whether the post passes as human.

- **Staccato.** Three or more consecutive short sentences, each closing on a period, each roughly the same length. Model output defaults to this shape and Hebrew readers clock it instantly.

  ```
  ✗  פיטרתי את הלקוח הכי גדול שלנו.
     כולם אמרו שאני משוגעת.
     הוא הכניס 40% מההכנסות.
     אבל הוא לקח 90% מהאנרגיה.
  ```

  Four lines, 4 to 6 words each, four periods. It reads like a drum machine.

  ```
  ✓  פיטרתי השבוע את הלקוח שהכניס 40% מההכנסות שלנו, מה שנשמע כמו החלטה של מישהי שלא יודעת לספור.
     הוא לקח 90% מהאנרגיה של הצוות.
  ```

  Long line, then short. The short one lands **because** the long one set it up. A short sentence is punctuation. Once every sentence is short, nothing is emphasised.

  Working rule: inside any block of 4 lines, at least one line runs 15+ words and at least one runs under 8. Never three same-length lines in a row.

- **Rule of three everywhere.** `מהיר, פשוט, אלגנטי` / "fast, simple, elegant". One filler triad costs the post its credibility. Use two items, or four, or make the third carry different weight.
- **Uniform paragraph length.** Every block exactly two lines reads generated. Break the pattern on purpose at least twice per post.

### Sentence shapes

- **"Not X but Y"** in all variants: ״זה לא X אלא Y״, ״זאת לא שאלה של X, זאת שאלה של Y״, "It's not about X, it's about Y".
- **Negation openers**: ״זה לא רק…״, ״לא כי…״, "This isn't just…". Banned as a first line and as a re-hook.
- **Em dash (—)**. Never, in either language. Use a period, a comma or parentheses.
- **״וזה בדיוק למה״** and its family: ״וזאת בדיוק הנקודה״, ״וזה בדיוק העניין״, "and that's exactly why".
- Rhetorical question the writer answers one line later. `למה? כי…`
- Sentences that announce the structure instead of using it: ״יש כאן שלוש נקודות״, "there are three things here".

### Vocabulary

- English: delve, leverage, unlock, realm, tapestry, game-changer, journey, dive deep, moreover, furthermore, "it is worth noting", "in today's world", "as we all know", "at the end of the day".
- Hebrew: ״בעולם הדינמי של היום״, ״אנו חיים בעידן״, ״המפתח להצלחה הוא״, ״בסופו של דבר״, ״ברצוני לחלוק״, ״חשוב לציין״, ״יתרה מזאת״, ״בשורה התחתונה״, ״לא פחות מ״.
- ״אנו״ when the voice owner is one person. Always ״אני״.

### Substance

- Any business result, revenue figure, customer name or metric the voice owner did not supply. Replace with `[מספר חסר, לאמת]`.
- Adjective stacks doing the job a specific noun should do. ״שיפור משמעותי ומרשים״ becomes a number over a period.
- A payoff the reader could have written themselves before opening the post.
- Anything too pretty or over the top. The register to aim for is a smart person talking across a table.

### Hebrew mechanics

Full rules in `references/hebrew.md`. Gate-level, these three are auto-fails:

- **Gender mixing.** One gender per post, decided in STEP 0. A single slip fails.
- **Wrong quote marks.** Hebrew gershayim ״…״, not "…" and not "…". And do not overuse them, quote marks on every third phrase is itself a tell.
- **Numbers as words.** 3, 40%, ₪1,500 in digits.

## LAYER 2. THE VOICE OWNER

The voice owner is whoever's name goes on the post. **By default that is you**, the person who installed this skill and writes their own LinkedIn. If you write on someone else's behalf, it is them, and you fill this block once per person you write for.

Of everything in this repo, this block changes the output most. It is also the only part that must be filled before the skill produces anything worth publishing. An empty Layer 2 gets you a competent stranger's writing with your name on it.

```
VOICE OWNER:           [you, or the person you write for]
REGISTER:              [executive / coach / expert / contrarian / warm / academic / edgy]
GENDER OF ADDRESS:     [Hebrew only]

WORDS I USE:
WORDS I NEVER USE:
SENTENCE LENGTH I FAVOUR:
HOW I OPEN A POST:
HOW I CLOSE A POST:
PUNCTUATION HABITS:    [arrows, ellipses, emoji policy, brackets]
ENGLISH-IN-HEBREW:     [terms I say in English and never translate]
RECURRING THEMES:
WHAT I REFUSE TO POST ABOUT:

CALIBRATION SAMPLES:
  1. [a full post actually written by the voice owner]
  2.
  3.
```

### How to fill it fast

Paste in three posts the voice owner actually wrote, ideally the three that performed best. Then read those three and fill the fields from what is on the page.

**Do not fill the fields from self-description.** Everybody describes their own writing as direct and no-nonsense, including the people whose posts open with ״בעולם הדינמי של היום״. The samples are evidence and the description is a wish.

Writing for yourself makes this easier and also easier to skip. Do it anyway. The gap between how you think you write and how you actually write is exactly the gap this skill exists to close, and you cannot see it from the inside.

If there are no samples at all, draft anyway and mark the output: *voice calibrated on description only, expect a rewrite pass*.

## KEEPING THE GATE ALIVE

The gate only works if it grows. Every time something in the output annoys you, add it the same day: Layer 1 if it is a machine tell that would bother any reader, Layer 2 if it is your own preference. ״וזה בדיוק למה״ arrived here that way, flagged three times before anyone wrote it down.
