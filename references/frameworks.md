# ANATOMY, FRAMEWORKS & CONTENT MIX

## Anatomy of a post: 5 layers

```
[1] HOOK        ←  first 1-2 lines. Stop the scroll. ≤140 chars.
[2] RE-HOOK     ←  one line. Deepens the loop. Earns the "see more" click.
[3] BODY        ←  the value. Story, framework, list or argument. ONE framework.
[4] PAYOFF      ←  what the reader now knows or can do.
[5] CTA         ←  soft. Question, comment prompt or save.
```

Any weak layer collapses the post. A strong hook on a thin body is worse than a weak hook, because it converts curiosity into distrust and the reader remembers the name.

## Body frameworks: pick ONE

Mixing two frameworks in one post is the most common structural failure and it is invisible to the writer. Pick one, name it in the brief, hold it to the end.

| Framework | Best for | Structure |
|-----------|----------|-----------|
| **HSV** (Hook, Story, Value) | Personal posts | Hook → Scene → Conflict → Resolution → Lesson |
| **AIDA** | Offers, persuasion | Attention → Interest → Desire → Action |
| **PAS** (Problem, Agitate, Solve) | Thought leadership | Problem → Make it hurt → Solution → Steps |
| **PSR** (Problem, Solution, Result) | Lead generation | Result → Problem → Approach → Detailed result → CTA |
| **Transformation** | Origin story | Humble start → Decision → Obstacles → Action → Outcome → Lesson |
| **Listicle / How-To** | Educational | Hook with N → 1. 2. 3. → Bonus → Soft CTA |
| **Contrast** | Reframes | ״אל תגידי X, תגידי Y״ → why it matters |
| **Villain / Conflict** | Calling out a system | Setup → Name the villain → Specific incidents → Boundary → Rhetorical close |

**SLAY** is the Vulnerability variant of HSV: Setback → Lesson → Application → Yield. The Yield beat is the one people drop, and without it a vulnerability post is just a complaint.

### Framework by post type, as a starting point

| Post type | Frameworks that fit |
|---|---|
| Curiosity | Contrast, Villain, PAS |
| Authority | Listicle, PSR, a named 3-step framework |
| Vulnerability | HSV, Transformation, SLAY |

Across a 3-post set, use three different frameworks. The same framework three times produces one post in three outfits, and the audience notices before the writer does.

## Content mix per voice owner, weekly

> ⚠️ **Every percentage in this section is tagged `[rule of thumb]`.** These splits come from running LinkedIn programs for founders, and none of them was measured on your audience. They are opening positions for week one. Section below explains how to replace them with real numbers, and you should, because a confident-looking table is the easiest thing in this repo to mistake for evidence.

Default Circulation Loop split, tuned for founder and personal-brand positioning:

- **40% Curiosity**, top of funnel magnet
- **40% Authority**, middle of funnel proof
- **20% Vulnerability**, trust glue

This replaces the generic 70-20-10 creator split, which assumes an educator with an audience already in place. [rule of thumb]

**Starting positions per voice owner type:** [rule of thumb, all rows]

| Voice owner type | Curiosity | Authority | Vulnerability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal brand / consultant (default) | 40% | 40% | 20% |
| Coach / educator | 20% | 60% | 20% |
| Edgy founder / contrarian voice | 50% | 30% | 20% |
| Corporate executive, less personal | 40% | 50% | 10% |
| Technical founder building in public | 30% | 60% | 10% |

Record the chosen mix in Layer 2 in `voice-gate.md` so the calendar stays consistent across sessions and across whoever is drafting.

### Replacing these numbers with real ones

After 20 posts, put the real data next to the table. Log each post's type, impressions and comments at a fixed interval, then compare where the volume went against where the engagement came from.

If Vulnerability runs at 20% of the calendar and produces half the comments, the table above was wrong here and the real data wins. Overwrite the row. A split that survives contact with 20 real posts is worth more than every rule of thumb in this file.
